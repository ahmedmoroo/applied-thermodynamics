Key 7 · Section 4 · Q&A (Çengel 9-95)

Real gas turbine (Brayton)

50 min

heat added heat rejected work (isentropic) actual path

How to recognise it Air, compressor + turbine, ηC and ηT given, flow in m³/min.

Recipe

  1. Compressor, ideal endT2s=T1rp(k−1)/k
  2. Compressor, real endT2=T1+T2s−T1ηC
  3. Turbine, ideal endT4s=T3(1rp)(k−1)/k
  4. Turbine, real end (here T4 is given → solve for T3)T4=T3−ηT(T3−T4s)
  5. Mass flow from volume flow (V̇ in m³/s)m˙=P1V˙1RT1
  6. Compressor powerW˙C=m˙cp(T2−T1)
  7. Turbine powerW˙T=m˙cp(T3−T4)
  8. Back work ratiorBW=W˙CW˙T
  9. Efficiencyηth=W˙T−W˙C[m˙cp(T3−T2)]

Use this problem's cp = 1.108 and k = 1.35, not 1.005 and 1.4.

Key 8 · Lec 3 · Regeneration Example 1

Regenerator

20 min

heat added heat rejected work (isentropic) actual path

How to recognise it Brayton + "regenerator" + effectiveness ε.

Recipe

  1. Same first four steps as Key 7 (k = 1.4)T2s=T1rp0.2857T4s=T3rp0.2857
  2. Real endsT2=T1+T2s−T1ηCT4=T3−ηT(T3−T4s)
  3. Check first: regenerator works only ifT4>T2
  4. Air leaving the regeneratorT5=T2+ε(T4−T2)
  5. Net work (regenerator changes nothing here)wnet=cp(T3−T4)−cp(T2−T1)
  6. Heat in now starts from T5qin=cp(T3−T5)
  7. Efficiencyηth=wnetqin

P₁ = 100 kPa is assumed for the table; only rp = 7 matters.

How to recognise it Simple Rankine, but ηT and ηP are given, and the power in MW.

Recipe

  2. Ideal pump workwp,s=v1(P2−P1)
  3. Real pump workwp,in=wp,sηP

Key 4 · Section 2 · Sheet 1, problem 49 (≈ Çengel 10-35)

Reheat: find the reheat pressure

45 min

heat added heat rejected work (isentropic) actual path

How to recognise it Two turbines + reheat, and the exit quality (x₆ = 0.95) is given instead of the reheat pressure.

Recipe

  1. Start from the end: entropy at the LPT exits6=sf+x6sfg
  2. LPT is isentropics5=s6
  3. Table 3 at T5: find the pressure where s = s5 (interpolate)P5=P4=Preheat
  4. HPT end (s4 = s3, at P4)h4=hf+x4hfg
  5. Pumpwp=v1(P2−P1)
  6. Heat in: boiler + reheaterqin=(h3−h2)+(h5−h4)
  7. Turbine work: HPT + LPTwT=(h3−h4)+(h5−h6)
  8. Efficiencyηth=wT−wpqin
  9. Heat rate of the boilerQ˙in=m˙qin
  10. Heat rate (kJ per kWh)HR=3600ηth

Key 5 · Section 2 · problem 50 = Sheet 2 · 10-46

Open feedwater heater

40 min

heat added heat rejected work (isentropic) actual path

How to recognise it Steam is bled from the turbine and MIXES with the feedwater (open FWH); two pumps.

Recipe

  1. Pump I (condenser → heater pressure)h2=h1+v1(PFWH−P1)
  2. Heater exit: sat. liquid at PFWH (Table 2)h3=hf@PFWH
  3. Pump II (heater → boiler pressure)h4=h3+v3(Pboiler−PFWH)
  4. Turbine states (all s = s5)h6atPFWHh7atPcond
  5. Heater balance (1 kg leaves)y·h6+(1−y)h2=h3
  6. Solve for yy=h3−h2h6−h2
  7. Heat in (1 kg through the boiler)qin=h5−h4
  8. Heat out (only 1 − y reaches the condenser)qout=(1−y)(h7−h1)
  9. Efficiencyηth=1−qoutqin

Closed feedwater heater

10 min

How to recognise it Same as Key 5, but the heater is CLOSED: streams do not mix, the drain is pumped forward.

Recipe

  1. Heater balancey(h7−h3)=(1−y)(h9−h2)
  2. Solve for yy=h9−h2[(h7−h3)+(h9−h2)]
  3. Mixing chamberh5=y·h4+(1−y)h9
  4. Heat inqin=h6−h5
  5. Heat outqout=(1−y)(h8−h1)
  6. Efficiencyηth=1−qoutqin

Carnot vs Rankine

10 min

How to recognise it "Compare Carnot and Rankine" between the same two pressures.

Recipe

  1. Temperatures in kelvin (Tsat at each pressure)T=Tsat+273.15

  3. Rankine: same steps as Key 1ηR=[(h3−h4)−wp]h3−h2

Key 9 · Lec 3 · Example 3

Intercooling + reheat + regenerator

10 min

How to recognise it Two compressors, two turbines, same pressure ratio per stage.

Recipe

  1. Each compressor stageT2=T4=T1rp,st(γ−1)/γ
  2. Each turbine stageT7=T9=T6rp,st(γ−1)/γ
  3. Works (two equal stages)wC=2cp(T2−T1)wT=2cp(T6−T7)

  5. Heat in: combustor + reheaterqin=cp(T6−T5)+cp(T8−T7)
  6. Efficiencyηth=wT−wCqin
